Lock your phone while studying

A website cannot block other apps. Only your phone can. Use these settings together with this page, which keeps the screen on and in full screen, and records every time you leave it.

Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Focus mode → pick the apps to pause (everything except Phone, WhatsApp and your study apps) → Turn on now. The menu name varies a little by brand. You can also use Settings → Security → App pinning to pin this page.
iPhone: Settings → Focus → Study (or Do Not Disturb) → Allowed Apps: Phone, WhatsApp and your study apps, and allow calls from your contacts. For a single-app lock use Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, then triple-click the side button on this page.

Before you open a call, WhatsApp or a study app, press Call / WhatsApp / study app so that time is not counted as a distraction.
